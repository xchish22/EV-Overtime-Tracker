Take-home estimate (2026 federal + BC tax tables)

Taxable income starts at your salary minus pension contributions: $137,677 − about $16,819 (your stub's $644.69 × 26.09 pays) = $120,858. Each overtime dollar is taxed at your top federal and BC rates, and CPP and EI come off only until they reach their 2026 maximums. Overtime is assumed not to be pensionable. Your actual payroll withholding on a single pay stub can differ, since payroll annualizes each pay. Your true tax is settled when you file.

About & recent updates

How versions are numbered (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH)
  • Patch (2.0.x): minimum change. Bug fixes, wording and small visual or calculation corrections. No new features and no change to saved data.
  • Minor (2.x.0): new features that work alongside everything already there. Existing entries and settings keep working unchanged.
  • Major (x.0.0): maximum change. Changes how data is stored or how existing features behave. Saved data is upgraded automatically on first open, and the notes say what changed.
